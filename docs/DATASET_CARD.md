# Job Market Intelligence Corpus

A multi-source, entity-resolved corpus of job postings, published as
denormalized Parquet shards. It is the data layer of the Job Market
Intelligence Platform; the API/forecasting/XAI layers are not built.

**Snapshot:** 2026-11-28 (latest posting date in the corpus: 2026-11-28).
**Rows:** 279,859. **Boards with data:** 255 (286 registered, 233 enabled).
**Columns:** 57, one row per posting.

This card describes what the snapshot actually contains, not what the pipeline
aspires to. The numbers are generated by `scripts/publish_metrics.py`; the
current table is in
https://github.com/sifatkarim870-cyber/job-market-intelligence/blob/main/reports/current_metrics.md.

## Files

```
shards/source=<source_code>/dt=<date>/part-0.parquet
state/*.parquet          # reference tables used to seed CI
dumps/*.dump             # pg_dump snapshots
```

One Parquet file per source in the current snapshot. `posting_date` is the
partition key; `posting_date_source` says whether it came from the publisher
(`source`) or from the first scrape date (`scrape_fallback`).

74,748 (26.7%), then Chinese (5.0%), Indonesian (4.6%), Spanish (4.2%), French
(3.0%), Georgian (2.5%), German (2.0%), Portuguese (1.9%) and others; 11.0%
carry no language.

## Known limitations

- **Not globally representative.** Iran dominates the corpus; 20.5% of rows
  have no resolved country. Do not use it for cross-country comparisons
  without weighting or a country-specific subset.
- **Employer identity is incomplete.** 48,215 rows (17.2%) still point at the
  historical CI-merge sentinel; the employer was not exported before
  2026-11-04 and cannot be recovered from the corpus. A further 6,709 rows
  (2.4%) are `Unknown employer`.
- **Dates.** 78,704 rows (28.1%) use the first scrape date because the
  publisher supplied none. Filter `posting_date_source = 'source'` for
  time-series work; same-day postings from dated boards are conservatively
  labelled `scrape_fallback` in the historical backfill.
- **Title classification** matches at the 0.55 cosine threshold against a
  2,046-title taxonomy. Average confidence is 0.788; at a stricter 0.70 cutoff
  coverage is 49.3% (138,032 rows), not 65.1%. The threshold is not calibrated
  against a labelled sample.
- **Skills** are a 434-term regex taxonomy over title + description. 71.6% of
  postings match.
- **Salary** covers 34.0% of postings with a disclosed amount. Only 26.3% of
  postings (77.3% of disclosed amounts) are USD-normalized: 21 currencies have
  static in-code rates (`_USD_CONVERSION_RATES`, dated 2026-11), and no per-row
  `fx_date` is stored. The `salary.currency_exchange_rates` table exists but is
  empty.
- **Locations**: 8,396 rows (3.0%) have no location. Multi-country/global
  boards are deliberately left null rather than guessed.
- **Deduplication**: same-source duplicate groups are 0 after the 2026-11-08
  collapse; 31 rows carry a cross-source `is_duplicate_of` flag and are not
  removed from the Parquet rows.
- **Provenance**: `translated_by` is NULL on 261,771 of 279,859 rows (93.5%;
  an older backfill did not stamp it); salary FX has no date;
  `title_classified_by` is set for the local title runs.

## Processing

1. Scrapers fetch/parse/validate/clean into a shared `CleanedJob` contract.
2. `core.jobs` is PostgreSQL 18, monthly range-partitioned by `posting_date`
   (61 partitions, 2024-08 through 2029-07, plus a default partition).
3. Company/location/salary resolution runs inline; titles/skills are batch jobs.
4. Descriptions are translated with NLLB-200 on Kaggle GPU; the overlay is
   applied by natural key.
5. Shards are rebuilt from the database of record and uploaded to Hugging Face.
